# white_calm-to-shocked_fullbody

Scrolls the phone calmly, eyes widen at ~4.5s, then jaw-drop shock from 6.5s to the end.

- **Background:** Plain white studio. AI cutout needed for new backgrounds.
- **Framing:** Full body, centred, fills more of the frame (feet ~y 1720, head ~y 400), so shrink it (fg_scale ≈ 0.68) to fit text.
- **Branding:** Black cap with photo logo, black tee that says "Keep".
- **Cutout:** AI cutout works well.
- **Beat:** **4.5s**: first surprise. **6.5s**: full jaw-drop. Punchline at 4.0–4.5s.
- **Good for:** Number reveals: "Photos taken today: 2,941 💀", "Photos: 118 GB".
- **Audio:** Has an audio track.
