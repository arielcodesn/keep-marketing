# desk_frustrated-to-defeated_branded

At an office desk, swiping angrily, slams the phone down at ~5.5s, then sits defeated.

- **Background:** Office desk with a lamp and plant (built-in scene).
- **Framing:** Waist up behind a desk; desk covers the lower body.
- **Branding:** Black cap and tee with the photo-and-heart logo.
- **Cutout:** AI cutout includes the office chair and is cut flat at the desk line. Use with a background that has a desk or counter.
- **Beat:** **2.5s**: anger starts. **5.5s**: slams the phone down. **8.5s**: defeated face.
- **Good for:** Can't-find-a-photo and iCloud-nag jokes: "Trying to find ONE photo of my ID…".
- **Audio:** Has an audio track.
